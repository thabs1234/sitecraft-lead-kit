# SiteCraft SA — Google Maps Lead Prospecting Playbook
*Built from the "I Tried Making $800 in 4 Hours with Google Maps" challenge, adapted for South African local businesses. Your offer: **R1,500 setup + R450/month**.*

---

## 0. The model in one line
Find small SA brick-and-mortar businesses with a **weak or unclaimed Google Business Profile**, pitch a done-for-you online presence (GBP setup + simple site), close at **R1,500 once-off + R450/mo**, deliver, then upsell.

The video proved the loop works in 4 hours with zero startup cost. We just swap cold-calls for **WhatsApp** (what SA owners actually answer) and US towns for **SA cities/towns**.

---

## 1. Validate the niche (5 min, free)
Same move as the video, SA-flavoured:
- **Google Trends** (`trends.google.com`) → search "Google Business Profile", "small business marketing South Africa", your city name + "business". Confirm steady interest.
- **Facebook / Google Maps density check**: search your target town for a trade (e.g. "plumber Polokwane"). If dozens of listings show weak profiles, the opportunity is real.
- Rule: only work towns where you can read/write the local language (English / Afrikaans / Zulu / Xhosa etc.).

## 4. Red-flag checklist — is this a GOOD prospect?
A business is worth contacting if it shows **2 or more** of these:

- [ ] Listing says **"Claim this business"** (owner never claimed the profile)
- [ ] **No website** link in the profile
- [ ] **Few / low reviews** (under ~20 reviews, or rating under 4.0)
- [ ] **Few photos** (under 5) or poor-quality images
- [ ] **Missing hours, phone, or address** incomplete
- [ ] **No posts / updates** in the last 3+ months
- [ ] **Weak category / description** (generic or blank)
- [ ] Ranks low when you search the trade + town

> Skip businesses that already look polished (100+ reviews, pro photos, active posts). They'll be hard to sell and don't need you yet.

## 5. Outreach — WhatsApp first (SA default)
SA owners answer WhatsApp far more than unknown calls. Use the **Click-to-Chat** link to message without saving the number:
`https://wa.me/27XXXXXXXXX?text=...` (replace 0 with 27, drop the leading 0).

```

your business doesn't have a claimed Google profile / website yet.

I help small businesses like yours get found — a done-for-you
Google Business Profile + simple website for a once-off R1,500,
then R450/month to keep it topped up.

Can I send you a free 2-minute look at what your listing could be?
No strings.
```

### Follow-up (if no reply in 2 days)
```
Hi {Name} 👋 just following up on my message. Even a basic Google
profile gets you showing up when someone nearby searches "{trade} {town}".
I'll send the free mini-audit either way — want me to?
```

### Cold-call script (for when you have a number and want speed)
```
Hi, my name is Thabang from SiteCraft SA. I help small {trade}
businesses in {town} show up on Google Maps when customers search.

I noticed your listing isn't claimed yet — that's free visibility

```
If they say "send info" → WhatsApp the mini-audit. If "not now" → "When's a good time to follow up?" → calendar it.

## 6. The free mini-audit (your closer)
Before asking for money, send a 1-page screenshot + notes showing:
1. Their current listing as customers see it (screenshot from Maps).
2. What's missing (red flags from §4).
3. A mock of the improved profile (claimed, correct info, 5–10 photos, posts, a "Book/Call" button).
4. The math: *"X% of {town} searches for {trade} — each one is a missed call today."*

This is the exact "free assessment" the video's prospects asked for. It builds trust and makes the R1,500 feel like a steal.

---

## 7. Close & price (from the video's result)
- Video closed: **2 × $250 + 1 × $200 = $700** in 4 hours.
- Your close: **R1,500 setup + R450/mo**. To hit R800-equivalent day-1 cash, you need ~1 setup closed; the R450s are the recurring engine.
- Negotiation tip (from video): he dropped one client $250→$200. Hold your R1,500 but offer a **first-month-free** or **"pay R1,200 if I don't get you 5 new reviews in 30 days"** style guarantee to unblock hesitant owners.

---

## 8. Deliver (so they stay + refer)
- Claim & verify their Google Business Profile.
- Fill every field: hours, services, description, correct map pin.
- Upload 8–10 real photos (ask them to send, or use stock + their logo).
- Add posts (offers, hours, "now open").
- Build the simple site (your R1,500 product) and link it.
- Monthly (R450): keep posts fresh, reply to reviews, add photos, report views/calls.
