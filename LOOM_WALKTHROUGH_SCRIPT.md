# Client Walkthrough — Loom script (record this, ~3 min)

A ready-to-read script for a short Loom/screen recording you send to prospects or
post on your SiteCraft SA socials. Talking-head + screen-share. Keep it casual, SA tone.

---

## [0:00–0:20] Hook (face to camera)
"Hey — quick one. If you run a small business in South Africa and someone Googles
you right now… can they actually find you? Half the businesses I check on Google Maps
aren't claimed, have no website, and show up on page 3. I built a free guide that
shows you exactly how to fix that in an afternoon — and I'll show you the tool I use."

## [0:20–0:50] Screen-share: the problem (Google Maps)
[Open maps.google.com, search "<trade> in <town>", e.g. "plumber Polokwane"]
"Look at this listing — no website, 3 reviews, 'Claim this business' still there.
That's free customers walking past every single day. The guide shows you the 8 red
flags to check on your own profile."

## [0:50–1:20] Screen-share: the free guide (PDF)
[Open sitecraft_sa_guide.pdf]
"Here's the free guide — step by step. Validate demand on Google Trends, take Google's
free 42-minute course, find weak listings, and the exact WhatsApp message that gets
owners to reply here in SA. No email needed, just grab it."

## [1:20–1:50] Screen-share: the landing page
[Open https://thabs1234.github.io/sitecraft-lead-kit/]
"This is where you get it — pop your WhatsApp, and the guide lands straight in your
inbox. Or you can just WhatsApp me. Totally free, no spam."

## [1:50–2:30] The offer (face to camera)
"Now — if you'd rather I just do it for you: I'll claim and optimise your Google
profile and build you a simple website for a once-off **R1,500**, then **R450 a month**
to keep it topped up — posts, reviews, photos, the lot. Most clients see more calls
in the first month."

## [2:30–2:55] CTA
"Grab the free guide with the link below, or WhatsApp me 'GOOGLE' and I'll send it
plus a free look at your own listing. I'm Thabang from SiteCraft SA — let's get you
found."

## [2:55–3:00] Outro
[Smile, end screen with WhatsApp + link]

---

### Recording tips
- Use Loom (loom.com) or your phone screen-record + face cam.
- Keep total under 3:30. Hook in first 5 seconds.
- Put the link + WhatsApp in the video description and as an on-screen card at the end.
- Post to: WhatsApp Status, Facebook (your town's community groups), Instagram Reels,
  and your SiteCraft portfolio.
- Variant for owners: same script but open THEIR listing live ("I looked you up…").
