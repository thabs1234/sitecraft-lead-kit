How to Make Money with Google Maps in South Africa

A free, step-by-step guide — adapted from the "$800 in 4 hours" Google Maps challenge for SiteCraft SA local businesses.

Your offer: R1,500 setup + R450 / month

This guide shows you exactly how to find small South African businesses with weak Google listings, pitch them a done-for-you online presence, and close at R1,500 once-off + R450/month. Zero startup cost. Works in a single afternoon.

1. Validate the idea (5 minutes, free)

  1. Go to trends.google.com. Search "Google Business Profile" and "small business marketing South Africa". Confirm steady interest over the past 5 years.
  2. Open Google Maps and search a trade in your town (e.g. plumber Polokwane). If dozens of listings look unfinished, the opportunity is real.
  3. Only target areas where you speak the local language (English / Afrikaans / Zulu / Xhosa).

Rule: only pursue a niche that isn't a "sinking ship."

2. Learn the skill for free (≈42 minutes)

Google runs a free "Google Business Profile" course on Skillshop (Google's own training platform). Take it so you can honestly say you're Google-trained and know how to claim, verify and optimise profiles. That credibility is what gets owners to trust you.

3. Find prospects on Google Maps

A

Pick your hunting ground

Start with smaller towns (less competition for attention): Polokwane, Nelspruit, Kimberley, Bloemfontein, Gqeberha/PE, Rustenburg, George, Upington. Later move to richer segments (dentists, accountants, attorneys, estate agents).

B

Run the searches

On maps.google.com, one town at a time:

"<trade> in <town>"   "<trade> near <town>"   "<trade> <suburb>, <town>"

Trade starter list: salon, beauty spa, nail tech, plumber, electrician, painter, pet groomer, car wash, takeaway, coffee shop, mechanic, tutor, photographer, baker, cleaning service.

C

Spot the weak profiles (red flags)

A business is worth contacting if it shows 2 or more of these:

  • Listing says "Claim this business" (never claimed)
  • No website link
  • Few / low reviews (under ~20, or under 4.0★)
  • Few photos (under 5) or poor images
  • Missing hours / phone / address
  • No posts in the last 3+ months
  • Weak or blank description / category
  • Ranks low when you search the trade + town

Skip polished businesses (100+ reviews, pro photos, active posts) — hard to sell, don't need you yet.

5. The free mini-audit (your closer)

Before asking for money, send a 1-page screenshot + notes showing:

  1. Their current listing as customers see it (screenshot from Maps).
  2. What's missing (the red flags from step 3C).
  3. A mock of the improved profile (claimed, correct info, 8–10 photos, posts, a "Call/Book" button).
  4. The math: "X% of {town} searches for {trade} — each one is a missed call today."

This is the exact "free assessment" prospects ask for. It builds trust and makes R1,500 feel like a steal.

6. Close & price

PRICING

Setup: R1,500 once-off (claim + optimise profile, build simple site, photos, posts).
Monthly: R450 (keep posts fresh, reply to reviews, add photos, report views/calls).

To hit day-1 cash fast, close 1 setup. The R450s are your recurring engine.
Unblock hesitant owners with a guarantee: "Pay R1,200 if I don't get you 5 new reviews in 30 days" or first month free.

Reference result from the challenge: closed 2 × $250 + 1 × $200 = $700 in 4 hours. Your R1,500 setup is the SA equivalent — and you keep the monthly income.

7. Deliver (so they stay & refer)

  1. Claim & verify their Google Business Profile.
  2. Fill every field: hours, services, description, correct map pin.
  3. Upload 8–10 real photos (ask them to send, or use stock + their logo).
  4. Add posts (offers, hours, "now open").
  5. Build the simple site (your R1,500 product) and link it.
  6. Monthly (R450): keep posts fresh, reply to reviews, add photos, report views/calls.
